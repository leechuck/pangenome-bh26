# Noncanonical C4 diagnostic-motif candidates

This is a sequence-candidate record, not a claim of a novel named or functional C4 allele. The canonical five-site C4A motif used by this assay is CGTGC; three training genes instead carry GGTGC. All retain a long-form HERV interval in the assembly audit. The inherited annotation calls them C4AL.

| Gene | Donor | Motif | Assembly form | Independent short-read evidence in this experiment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HG02109_1__C4_1 | HG02109 | GGTGC | C4?L | No read experiment for this donor |
| NA19700_1__C4_1 | NA19700 | GGTGC | C4?L | 16 deduplicated Q20 exact-marker fragments; OTHER dosage call 1 |
| apr008_1__C4_1 | apr008 | GGTGC | C4?L | No read experiment for this donor |

The NA19700 read evidence is from public WGS and supports the altered diagnostic sequence relative to the assembly, but it does not phase the motif to the long-form insertion or chromosome. The graph sequence itself is derived from the assemblies and is not an independent replication. Exact fragment matching does not establish the protein consequence, expression, disease relevance, or novelty in an external allele database.

These three genes were identified during development. Their discovery and the NA19700 read check must not be presented as held-out validation. Independent full-gene sequence confirmation, coding consequence annotation and comparison against curated C4 sequence resources remain appropriate follow-up work.
